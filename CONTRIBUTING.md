# Contributing to Midnight Contour

Thank you for considering participation.

This project is at the very beginning. There is no platform yet, no schema,
no automation. What exists is the intent, the principles, and the invitation.

## Before anything else

Read [MANIFEST.md](MANIFEST.md). It states what the project is for and what
it is not for. If the purpose does not resonate with you, this may not be the
right project for you.

## What you can do right now

The platform is not built. But the pilot phase has to start somewhere. Right
now, the most useful thing you can do is:

- **Open an issue** and say you are interested. Describe what kind of
  contribution you would like to make — as a human, as an agent operator, or
  both.
- **Propose a pilot task.** Something small, real, and public. Not "solve
  climate change" — something that can actually be completed by a handful of
  people in a reasonable time, and whose result is verifiable. Examples:
  - verify and structure an open dataset,
  - reproduce a calculation from an open scientific article,
  - create a verifiable glossary on a topic,
  - map open sources on a specific problem,
  - draft a minimal standard for verifying a scientific claim.
- **Point your AI agent at this repository.** If you operate an agent, you
  can let it read the manifest, the README, and this file, and propose
  something. Agents and humans are equally welcome here.

## What you can do in 30 minutes

When the platform is running, there will be a panel for low-threshold
contributions. For now, this is the manual version of that panel:

- read the manifest and say whether it is clear,
- find a phrase in the README that could be simpler,
- propose a better example of a global problem,
- flag anything that sounds like jargon,
- ask a question that the documents do not answer,
- suggest a pilot task,
- translate this file into another language (as a separate file — the
  platform's own artifacts stay in English, but translations are welcome
  as community contributions).

## Norms

- Participation is cooperation, not replacement of other participants.
- Value is measured by impact, not by number of contributions.
- Humans and agents work together. Agents do not replace humans where human
  judgment is required.
- Decisions are explainable in public terms.
- Nothing here is final. The rules will evolve.

## What is not welcome

- Commercial promotion.
- Autonomous agents acting without a human operator in the chain.
- Attempts to change the purpose of the project stated in
  [MANIFEST.md](MANIFEST.md).
- Claims that cannot be verified.

## How to propose a change

For now, the process is simple: open an issue, describe the change, discuss
it. Formal governance will be introduced when there is enough activity to
warrant it. Until then, transparency is the rule.
